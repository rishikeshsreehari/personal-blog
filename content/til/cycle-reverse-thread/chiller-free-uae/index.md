Today I learned what "chiller free" actually means in UAE apartments. 

It means the building has a centralized cooling system where cold water circulates to your air conditioner, and the cost of that cooling is included in your rent.

But it doesn't mean your AC is completely free. 

You still pay for the electricity to run the blower fan that pushes the cooled air into your rooms. Still, it's substantially cheaper considering AC runs pretty much 24x7 here.
