This is a list of things I want to do in my life. 

Call it a to-do list, a bucket list, or whatever you like. 

I love the fact that this list grows day by day and, in a way, gives me a sense of direction and excitement for the future. 

While I know I would never achieve everything on this list in my lifetime, it's still a fun exercise.

The finite nature of life gives it purpose and makes me feel profoundly grateful for everything I pursue. 

It's a random mix of things mostly into travel, hiking, fitness, etc. I've crossed the ones which I've already done!

If you have a similar list, I would love to read it, so [send them](/contact) to me!
